Hand Design gives Chrome a theme system with 24 themes: One UI, iOS Liquid Glass, Material You, Windows 11, macOS, Nothing, Night Drive, Retro 98, Terminal, Cyberpunk, Synthwave, High Contrast and more. A theme changes the new tab page and every tool the extension adds, so they look like one piece.

New tab
- Large clock and date, a search box with suggestions from Google and your history, site tiles.
- Wallpapers: each theme has its own, animated ones included, or use your own picture.
- Clock, wallpaper and search box can each come from a different theme (Studio → Theme details).

Studio
- All settings on one page with a live preview of the new tab: themes, presets, accent color, light or dark mode, corners, fonts.
- Save settings to a file and load them on another computer.

Quick panel
- Click the Hand Design icon to switch theme, mode and wallpaper without leaving the page.

Now Bar and call bar
- Now Bar shows what is playing in any tab, with pause, next and previous. It ignores short site sounds and hides while a video is in picture-in-picture.
- The call bar appears when a site uses your microphone or camera and lets you mute them with Alt+Shift+M.

Tab switcher
- Alt+Q opens a grid of open tabs with previews.

Notes
- Markdown notes in a folder you choose, compatible with Obsidian: live preview, folders, search, [[links]] and backlinks, #tags, callouts, tables, checklists, images, graph view, daily note and templates.
- Import from Notion (Markdown & CSV export), Evernote (.enex), Google Keep (Google Takeout) and any folder of .md files.

Documents and editors
- Markdown files open formatted, with a table of contents and editing in place.
- CSV and TSV files open as an editable table instead of downloading.
- Image editor: crop, rotate, arrows, frames, text, blur, compression and format change. Right-click any image → Edit image.
- PDF viewer in the theme's colors.

10 interface languages: English, Russian, Ukrainian, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Polish, Turkish, Chinese.

Notes for local files: to open .md, .csv and .pdf files from your computer, turn on "Allow access to file URLs" on the extension's details page.

Chrome does not let extensions restyle its own tabs and toolbar. The free Hand Design installer for Windows (GitHub) can paint the browser frame in the theme's color.
